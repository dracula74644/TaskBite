BITE ORIGINALS — THREE-LINE HUMAN VOICE AUDITION

Recommended approach
Use one human performer for the whole pack. First try these three lines
with a friend or your own voice. Choose a delivery you enjoy hearing
repeatedly, then record the full 24-line script. A voice actor is an option
if those samples do not have the personality you want.

Character
An amused sidekick who wants you to succeed and notices your excuses.
Conversational, dry, and warm. Let the joke land with a short pause.
Encouragement should sound pleased; teasing should sound playful.
Use your natural voice and an original character, without imitating a
specific game performer. No shouting, humiliation, or threatening delivery.

Record two takes of each sample: one understated, one more animated.
Keep the words unchanged so we can compare the performances.

1. A task was added — CREATED/01.wav
   "Another side quest. Your collection is getting suspicious."
   Direction: curious approval, then a small smile on "suspicious."

2. A task was completed — COMPLETED/01.wav
   "You finished! I had a whole roast prepared."
   Direction: pleased surprise, followed by mock disappointment.

3. A deadline was missed — OVERDUE/01.wav
   "Your deadline called. I said you were buffering."
   Direction: dry and conspiratorial, as if covering for a friend.

Recording
A quiet room and phone microphone are enough for the first audition.
Keep the microphone distance consistent. Leave a little silence before
and after each line. Avoid music, reverb effects, and clipped/distorted
speech. Aim for about 2–5 seconds per line at a natural pace.
Keep the original recordings. Use PCM WAV when the recorder supports it;
do not rename a compressed file to .wav.

Try it in the app
Sidekick > Voice settings: select the matching situation, record or import
the take, then preview it at your usual listening volume. Enable "Use my
recordings" to hear it on the corresponding action. Unrecorded situations
continue using the selected existing human pack.

Selection
Listen on the phone speaker, including a few repeated plays. Pick the take
with clear words, natural timing, and humor that remains pleasant after
the novelty wears off. If both deliveries feel tiring, soften the delivery
before recording the remaining lines.

Full pack
Use recording-script.txt for all 64 lines across 16 situations. Save the
chosen take under each listed filename. The packaging tool needs all 64
WAV files and a performer name; it normalizes recordings and creates the
optional Bite Originals pack. It does not create a human performance.
