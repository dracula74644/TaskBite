- Search tasks, checklist steps, and history.
- Break tasks into small checklists. Recurring tasks start with fresh unchecked steps.
- Choose Later today, with Undo, alongside Tomorrow.

- Preserve previously installed personal packs on the phone across later public updates.
- Add compact-screen and large-text checks, plus a separate Bite Test Lab for phone tests.
